Delete your WealthOS account

You can delete your account and all its data at any time, from the app or by email.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open WealthOS and unlock it.
  2. Go to Setup → Security.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete my account.
  4. Read what will be deleted. If you want a copy, tap Back up first to save an Excel or backup file.
  5. Tap Continue to delete, type DELETE and enter your account password.
  6. Tap Delete my account forever and confirm. Keep the internet on until you see the login screen.

Option 2: By email

If you cannot open the app or forgot your password, send an email to wealthos747@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my WealthOS account".

Send it from the email address you use to sign in (or include the mobile number you sign in with). We may reply to confirm it is really you before deleting.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email or mobile number and password) and any mobile number added for OTP login.
  • All entries: income, expenses, savings, investments and transfers.
  • Accounts, categories, budgets, Money Plan and settings.
  • Savings and investments (SIPs, FD, RD, PPF, gold and others), loans, repeating bills, future needs, goals, insurance policies and business entries.
  • All automatic backups stored in your account.
  • On the phone used for deletion: your PIN, saved choices, scheduled reminders, widget data and the local backup copy.

Not deleted: files you exported and saved or shared yourself (for example an Excel file sent to WhatsApp or Drive). Please delete those yourself if you want. Uninstalling the app removes its data from that phone.

How long it takes

  • In the app: immediately, while you wait.
  • By email: within 7 days of your request (at most 30 days). We will reply when it is done.
  • Residual copies in Google's internal system backups are cleared by Google on its normal schedule. We keep nothing after deletion.
