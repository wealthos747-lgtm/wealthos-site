Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

In short: RupeeFlow is a personal money tracker. You type in your own money details. They are stored in your own private space in Google Firebase, linked to your login, and only you can open them. We show no ads and we do not sell your data. We never read your contacts, photos or location. Optional features, all off until you turn them on, can read your bank SMS on your phone, read a bank statement you pick, listen when you tap the mic, and use Google's Gemini AI to answer questions about your entries (see section 5). You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains how the RupeeFlow Android app (package com.suresh.wealthos, "the app", "we") handles your information. RupeeFlow is made by an independent developer in Chennai, India. Contact: wealthos747@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address (or a mobile number used as your login name) and a password, to create and sign in to your account. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password ourselves.
  • Mobile number, only if you choose to add it for OTP login (Setup → Security). Firebase sends the OTP by SMS.
  • Your name, only if you type it in the app (used for the greeting on Home).

Money information you enter

Everything you add yourself: income and expense entries, accounts and balances, categories and budgets, your Money Plan, savings and investments (SIPs, mutual funds, FD, RD, PPF, gold and similar), loans, repeating bills, future needs, goals, insurance policies, business entries and app settings. You can also import entries from Excel/CSV files or backup files you pick.

The app does not connect to your bank and does not ask for bank logins, passwords, card numbers or UPI PINs. Please do not type such details into notes. Bank SMS reading and statement import are optional and described in section 5.

Technical information

Google Firebase, which runs our login and database, processes technical data such as IP address and device/app identifiers to sign you in securely, prevent abuse and keep the service working. We do not use any analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your data synced across your phones.
  • To show your reports: monthly flow, budgets, net worth, Money Plan and reminders.
  • To make automatic backups of your data inside your own account.
  • To reply when you email us.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or credit decisions, and we do not sell it.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase) in a private area tied to your account (users/<your id>). Security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or write it. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by Google. A copy is also kept on your phone so the app works offline; it syncs when you are back online.

Automatic backups: once a week the app saves a full copy of your data inside your own account (the latest 8 are kept) and the latest copy on your phone. These backups are deleted together with your account.

4. On your phone

  • PIN and fingerprint lock: your 4-digit PIN is stored only on your phone in Android's encrypted secure storage. Fingerprint / face unlock is handled by Android; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications (optional): reminders for daily entries, bill days and future needs are scheduled on the phone itself. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Home-screen widget (optional): shows a short summary (safe to spend, budget left, net worth) only if you add the widget.
  • Recent-apps preview is hidden so your numbers are not visible in the app switcher.
  • Exports: Excel, CSV, PDF and backup files are created only when you ask, and go only where you choose to share them.

5. Optional AI and bank automation

These features are off until you turn them on in Setup → AI & bank automation, and you can turn them off at any time. Some may not be in every version of the app.

AI answers, insights and auto categories (Google Gemini)

  • When you ask a question, open monthly insights, use voice entry, let AI pick categories or read a statement, the app sends the details needed for that answer to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic: entry dates, amounts, types, category and account names, notes and the question itself.
  • We ask for your consent before the first AI use. We never send your login password, PIN, bank passwords or full SMS text to the AI.
  • Google processes this data to give the answer under the Gemini API terms. While the app uses Google's no-cost tier, Google may use these requests to improve its products and people at Google may review them. Do not put very private details in notes if you are not comfortable with this.
  • RupeeFlow does not keep a separate copy of your questions or answers outside your device and account.

Microphone (voice questions and voice entry)

The app records your voice only while you tap the mic. The short recording is sent to Google Gemini (or, if AI is off, to your phone's own speech service) to understand what you said, then deleted from the phone. We do not store recordings.

Bank SMS (Android permission: read SMS)

  • If you turn this on and allow the permission, the app reads your SMS inbox on your phone to find debit and credit messages from banks.
  • Messages from people, OTPs, offers, reminders and anything not from a bank sender are skipped. The full SMS text never leaves your phone and is never sent to the AI.
  • From each bank message we keep only the amount, date, payee name, bank name and the last 4 digits of the account or card, saved as an entry in your own account. New entries wait for your approval unless you turn on Smart approve for shops you have approved before.
  • You can turn it off in the app or remove the SMS permission in Android settings at any time.

Bank statement import

When you pick a statement file (PDF, Excel or CSV), it is opened on your phone. A statement password is used only to open the file and is never saved. The statement text is sent to Google Gemini to read the transaction rows, and you check the rows before anything is added.

App verification (Firebase App Check)

To stop others from misusing our AI access, Firebase App Check (with Google Play Integrity for Play Store installs) checks that requests come from the genuine RupeeFlow app. Google processes device and app integrity signals for this.

7. Sharing

We do not share your personal or financial data with third parties. Your data is processed only by our service providers needed to run the app: Google Firebase (login, database and App Check), Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic (only for the optional AI features in section 5) and Google Play (app delivery and in-app updates). We would disclose information only if required by Indian law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and change all your data in the app at any time.
  • Export it as Excel, CSV or a full backup file (Setup → Backup & export).
  • Delete your account and all its data: in the app, Setup → Security → Delete my account, or by emailing wealthos747@gmail.com from your account email. Details: Delete your account.

9. Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data and login are removed from our live database immediately (in-app) or within 7 days of your email request (at most 30 days). Residual copies in Google's internal system backups are cleared by Google on its normal schedule.

10. Children

RupeeFlow is meant for adults (18+) managing their own money. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Security

We use Firebase security rules, encrypted connections, an app lock with PIN/biometrics and a hidden recent-apps preview. No method is 100% secure; please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
