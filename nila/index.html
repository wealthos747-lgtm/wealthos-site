ROI · ROAS · Incrementality

Know what a campaign, discount or listing boost really earned, beyond what would have happened anyway.

Incremental = campaign revenue − (baseline × (1 + market growth)). Market growth is how much a similar group grew with no campaign. Leave baseline blank for plain ROAS and ROI.

The period before the campaign is the same number of days right before the start date. Totals are compared per day, so different lengths are fair.
